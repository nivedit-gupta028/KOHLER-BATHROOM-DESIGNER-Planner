Innovation pitch

Constraint-aware generative merchandising.

The opportunity

Bathroom planning is fragmented across spreadsheets, product pages, floor-plan sketches, and disconnected visualizers.

The innovation

The system does not merely recommend attractive products. It resolves a collection that is budget-aware, space-aware, category-aware, placement-aware, theme-aware, and water-aware.

Why it matters

Computational planning and human judgment meet in one loop. Users understand why a collection works, then experience it spatially through the digital twin.

Design intent in. A buildable bathroom direction out.

Expansion path

  • Saved and comparable layouts
  • Client-ready PDF/image export
  • Accessibility and code checks
  • Richer finish intelligence
